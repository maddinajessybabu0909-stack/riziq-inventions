- Add a new image gallery section to the About page using the eight supplied RIZIQ event, classroom, training, and collaboration photos.
- Match the homepage Services carousel proportions and controls, while showing two evenly sized image cards at a time on larger screens and one on mobile.
- Advance automatically every 3.5 seconds, loop continuously, and keep previous/next arrows plus animated progress indicators.
- Use a consistent crop, a normal gap between photos, concise accessible captions, and no distracting hover effects.
